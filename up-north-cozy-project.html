Up North & Cozy

Shopify E-Commerce Store

An online storefront created for my small business, featuring handmade gifts, seasonal products, and a warm, welcoming shopping experience.

01 / THE PROJECT

Project Overview.

Up North & Cozy is my small business offering handmade and seasonal products, including heat bags, simmer jars, hot chocolate gifts, and digital products.

I created an online storefront using Shopify to showcase products, organize collections, and provide customers with a simple shopping experience.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My Role.

As the business owner, I was responsible for setting up and customizing the Shopify store, organizing products, writing product descriptions, and creating the overall shopping experience.

I worked with Shopify's Craft theme, configured product listings and collections, and set up features including gift cards and promotional discounts.

03 / REFLECTION

What I Learned.

Building my own Shopify store gave me hands-on experience working with an e-commerce platform and understanding how products, collections, and store settings work together.

I also gained experience with theme customization, organizing content, and designing a storefront that reflects a consistent brand identity.
